Advancing Underwater Image Enhancement & Computer Vision

Research Scholar @ AR/VR Lab, CICPS, IIT Guwahati

I’m Pranjali Singh, a Research Scholar at IIT Guwahati, working at the intersection of Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Intelligent Image Processing. My research focuses on underwater image enhancement, where I develop robust enhancement techniques using Transformers, Deep CNNs, and Mamba-based architectures to improve visual quality and restore information in challenging underwater environments.
My work aims to bridge advanced AI research with practical computer vision applications, with research contributions published in reputed international journals and conferences.

About My Research

Research Philosophy

My research focuses on developing efficient and intelligent deep learning techniques for underwater image enhancement, addressing challenges such as color distortion, haze, low contrast, and image degradation. I aim to design robust architectures that balance image quality and computational efficiency for practical underwater imaging applications.

My work includes DARE-UI, a degradation-aware framework incorporating classification and adaptive enhancement, and MSTR-Net, a multi-stage Transformer-based architecture for efficient feature extraction and image restoration. Currently, I am exploring DeltaNet-inspired Transformer architectures, focusing on efficient attention mechanisms and gated feature interactions.
